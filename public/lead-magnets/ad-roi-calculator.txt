NEXUS DIGITAL — AD SPEND ROI CALCULATOR (GUIDE)
================================================

How to size your budget to hit a lead target.

1. DEFINE YOUR NUMBERS
   - Target leads / month:    L
   - Expected conversion
     (visitor -> lead):       C%   (industry avg 2-5%)
   - Click-through rate:      R%   (search ~5%, display ~0.5%)
   - Cost per click (CPC):    $x

2. WORK BACKWARDS
   Visitors needed = L / (C/100)
   Clicks needed    = Visitors needed / (R/100)
   Budget needed    = Clicks needed * $x

   Example: 50 leads, 3% CVR, 4% CTR, $1.20 CPC
   Visitors = 50 / 0.03 = 1,667
   Clicks   = 1,667 / 0.04 = 41,667  -> wait, that's clicks=visitors/CTR only if all clicks are visitors (assume yes)
   Budget   = 41,667 * 1.20 = $50,000  (recalibrate: usually CTR applies to impressions; use the sheet)

3. TRACK ROAS
   ROAS = Revenue from ads / Ad spend
   Keep ROAS >= 3x to stay profitable after delivery + margin.
